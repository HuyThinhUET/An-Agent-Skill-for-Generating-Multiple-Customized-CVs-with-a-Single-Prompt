# MABe Mouse Behavior Detection

## Project context

Team project for a mouse social-action-recognition challenge. The task is frame-level, multi-label behavioral-segment prediction from pose-tracking data.

## Technical evidence

- Engineered FPS-aware temporal, kinematic, and social-interaction features from pose data.
- Combined LightGBM, XGBoost, and CatBoost in an ensemble.
- Used stratified subsampling, cross-validation, adaptive thresholds, and temporal post-processing.

## Result boundaries

The team placed 53rd of 1,413 teams on the public leaderboard with a frame-level F-beta score of 0.481. This is a team result; do not claim sole ownership of the feature engineering or ensemble.
